The challenge

Why great projects fail to start

Finding the right collaborators across departments is fragmented, leading to imbalanced skill sets and stalled initiatives.

  • Skills are hard to discover across campus departments.
  • Teams lack balanced roles between engineering and design.
  • No transparent application workflow with transactional integrity.
Our solution

One campus platform for balanced teams

A single place to showcase verifiable proficiencies, discover relevant projects, and manage team rosters.

01. Profile

List technical and soft skills with 1–5 proficiency ratings.

02. Match

Rank candidates transparently based on required role requirements.

03. Form

Approve join requests with single-transaction database integrity.
